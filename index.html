// the challenge

Engineering the brain's immune system

NeuroHack Paris brings together the best early-career researchers in Europe for an intensive weekend at the frontier of neuro-immunology. Real datasets, real compute, real jury.

Theme · Neuro-Immunology

From Microglia in ALS to Glioblastoma heterogeneity and digital phenotyping of inflammation-driven behaviour — three real-world challenges defined by our research partners. Build a tool, propose a bold therapy, or design a validation study. Pitch to a jury of VCs, pharma researchers, and academics on Sunday evening.

// challenge subjects

Three real problems

Teams pick one challenge and work on it across the full weekend. All problems come from our research and industry partners.

01
Servier × ICM

Microglia in ALS

Investigate microglial dynamics in ALS progression using public transcriptomics datasets. Proposed by Julien Verdier's lab at Paris Brain Institute in partnership with Servier.

See full brief
02
Paris Brain Institute

NeuroOncology & Glioblastoma

Explore spatial and cellular heterogeneity in Glioblastoma. Map the tumour immune microenvironment using single-cell and spatial transcriptomics.

See full brief
03
Digital Phenotyping

Wearable Biomarkers and Inflammatory Subtypes in Psychiatric Disease: Toward Preventative Psychiatry

Can passive wearable signals and an engaging self-report layer specifically detect cytokine-driven "sickness behavior" — and become an early-warning triage product?

See full brief
// two tracks

Choose your approach

Every team picks one challenge and one track. Both tracks work on all three problems.

AI / BIOINFORMATICS TRACK
Build something that runs

Deliver a working computational output over the weekend. AWS GPU compute and Jupyter notebooks with a built-in coding agent are provided. Use public omics datasets or bring your own ideas.

OUTPUT → Novel biomarker pipeline · Digital phenotyping prototype · Omics analysis · Closed-loop system
BLUE SKY TRACK
Define what nobody has asked yet

No code required. Propose a bold new research direction — a gene therapy approach, a new delivery mechanism, an untested hypothesis. Judged on scientific ambition, feasibility, and originality.

OUTPUT → Research proposal · Novel hypothesis · Translational or clinical roadmap
