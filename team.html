About Nucleate

The global network for the next generation of biotech leaders

Nucleate is a non-profit, volunteer-led organisation dedicated to empowering PhD students and post-docs to build transformational biotech companies. Through its global network of chapters, Nucleate connects early-career scientists with the mentorship, community, and resources they need to turn bold scientific ideas into real ventures — whether in academia, industry, or entrepreneurship.

NeuroHack Paris 2026 is organised by the Nucleate Paris chapter, part of this global network spanning 36 regions across 22 countries.

// sponsors & partners

Who makes this possible

NeuroHack Paris 2026 is made possible by the generous support of our sponsors and partners, each bringing unique expertise and resources to the event.

Main Sponsor

Servier

Servier is an independent international pharmaceutical group driven by the ambition of therapeutic progress for patients. With a strong research focus on oncology, cardiovascular, and neuroscience diseases, Servier brings both scientific expertise and industry mentorship to NeuroHack Paris. Their team will be present throughout the event as mentors and jury members, and they have proposed one of the three challenge subjects — investigating microglial dynamics in ALS — directly from their active research pipeline.

Venue Partner

Brain & Mind

Brain & Mind is the Greater Paris Neuroscience Cluster, spanning brain disorders, mental health, and sensory disorders. As the venue partner for NeuroHack Paris 2026, Brain & Mind provides its state-of-the-art incubator space — a dedicated environment at the intersection of neuroscience research and biotech entrepreneurship. Their ecosystem of startups, researchers, and investors is a natural home for an event built around translating bold ideas into real solutions.

Compute Partner

Amazon Web Services (AWS)

AWS is providing the computational infrastructure that powers the AI track at NeuroHack Paris. Teams in the AI / Bioinformatics track will have access to GPU compute resources and shared Jupyter notebook environments — the same setup AWS deployed for the Genopole hackathon — allowing participants to run real machine learning pipelines, process omics datasets, and prototype digital health tools without any local compute constraints. A built-in coding agent will also be available directly inside the notebook environment throughout the weekend.
