// format & schedule

How NeuroHack works

A 36-hour sprint from Friday evening to Sunday evening — structured enough to be productive, open enough to be bold. Here's what to expect.

// how it works

From application to award

1

Apply

Fill in the form — solo or as a team. 40 participants selected from across Europe.

2

Build your team

One week before, connect with other participants on Slack or Discord and form your team around a challenge.

3

Fork & get ready

Read the challenge briefs, explore starter notebooks, and fork the repo before you arrive.

gh repo fork hackathonparisnucleate-star/neurohack-2026
4

Hack Fri → Sun

36 hours on-site. AWS compute, on-site mentors from Servier and ICM. Push hard.

5

Pitch & win

Sunday afternoon: 5-minute pitch to the jury. Best teams win prizes and incubator access.
